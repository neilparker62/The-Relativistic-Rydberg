3. Mathematical Integrity of the Derivation

The mathematical structure relies on standard physical constants: the elementary charge (\(e\)), electron rest mass (\(m_0\)), speed of light (\(c\)), and fine-structure constant (\(\alpha\)).

The velocity wave equation \(v(t) = c\sin(\frac{Ft}{m_0 c})\) integrates to spatial displacement \(x(t)\). A binomial expansion of the core energy relation yields:

$$\mu c^2 \sqrt{1 - \frac{Z^2\alpha^2}{n^2}} \approx \mu c^2 - \frac{\mu c^2 Z^2 \alpha^2}{2 n^2}$$

This expansion recovers the classical non-relativistic Rydberg expression as a limiting case, demonstrating structural consistency with established low-energy formulas.
