$$\Delta n(\Delta \omega) \approx 1 - \frac{N e^2}{2 \epsilon_0 m_e \omega_0} \left[ \frac{\Delta \omega}{(\Delta \omega)^2 + (\gamma/2)^2} \right]$$

$$\Delta \Phi(\omega) = \frac{\omega L}{c} \Delta n(\omega)$$
