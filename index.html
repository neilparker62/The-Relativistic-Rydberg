Executive Summary

The Relativistic Rydberg monograph establishes a deterministic, real-space geometric framework for bound atomic systems. By grounding electron dynamics in a pure Lorentz mass variant of Newton's Second Law (\(F = m_{\text{rel}} \cdot a\)) and a Pythagorean quadrature relation between orbital kinetic energy and transverse radiant wave energy (\(hf\)), the model resolves foundational quantum paradoxes without invoking statistical abstractions. This repository hosts the complete derivation, appendices, and high-precision validation datasets matching empirical benchmarks across Hydrogen and Deuterium.
