1. Introduction to the Compendium

This compendium gathers a multi-part series of theoretical monographs and technical scripts detailing an alternative, semi-classical worldview of hydrogenic atomic systems. By replacing abstract wave clouds with real-space relativistic trajectories derived from a pure Lorentz mass variant of Newton's Second Law (F = mrel · a), this framework transforms traditional quantum paradoxes into concrete geometric invariants.

To preserve the deep precision of the analytical calculations, the attosecond timing profiles, the solenoidal magnetic corrections, and the proposed experimental validation protocols without exceeding individual manuscript formatting capacities, the complete research body has been organized into a primary paper supported by specialized volumes. This index serves as the centralized repository linking the complete standalone library.
