Executive Summary & Overview

The Relativistic Rydberg formula provides a robust, highly accurate mathematical framework that can be utilized anywhere the traditional classical Rydberg formula is applied. By incorporating rigorous relativistic dynamics, the formula yields spectral calculations and ionization potentials that achieve orders-of-magnitude greater precision across hydrogenic systems.

As detailed below, the classical Rydberg expression emerges naturally as a direct, one-term binomial expansion of the Relativistic Rydberg formula, demonstrating seamless structural continuity with established methods while delivering exceptional predictive accuracy.

1. Symmetry and Convergence with the Dirac Equation

The relativistic energy eigenvalue spectrum for a single-electron atom derived from the Dirac equation incorporates the principal quantum number \(n\) and the relativistic angular momentum integer \(k\):

$$E_{n,j} = \mu c^2 \left[ 1 + \left( \frac{Z\alpha}{n - |k| + \sqrt{k^2 - Z^2\alpha^2}} \right)^2 \right]^{-1/2}$$

Within the Relativistic Rydberg framework, applying a classical-relativistic quadrature relation between orbital kinetic energy and transverse radiant wave energy yields the streamlined expression:

$$E_n = \mu c^2 \sqrt{1 - \frac{Z^2\alpha^2}{n^2}}$$

Under the specific boundary conditions where spin and angular momentum quantum numbers in the Dirac equation are maximized for a given shell (\(l_{\text{max}} = n - 1\) and \(j_{\text{max}} = l + 1/2\)), the Dirac integer satisfies \(k = n\). Under this constraint, the multi-term expression \(n - |k|\) in the Dirac denominator vanishes to zero, and the general Dirac energy relation reduces algebraically to the Relativistic Rydberg expression. This demonstrates formal mathematical alignment between the geometric derivation and the Dirac eigenvalue spectrum under maximum angular momentum configurations.

3. Connection to Classical Limits (Binomial Expansion)

The classical Rydberg formula widely used in standard calculations is not replaced, but rather understood as a natural limiting case. By applying a one-term binomial expansion to the core square-root expression of the Relativistic Rydberg formula:

$$\mu c^2 \sqrt{1 - \frac{Z^2\alpha^2}{n^2}} \approx \mu c^2 - \frac{\mu c^2 Z^2 \alpha^2}{2n^2}$$

the traditional non-relativistic expression drops out cleanly. This establishes that the formula retains full structural compatibility with historical baselines while offering enhanced precision.

4. Physical Cohesiveness & Framework Summary

By connecting classical dynamics with relativistic motion, the framework provides a clear, self-consistent computational method for analyzing atomic transitions, helical electron trajectories, and magnetic invariants such as the Bohr magneton (\(\mu_B\)).
