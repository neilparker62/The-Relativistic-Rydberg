Executive Summary

The Relativistic Rydberg framework establishes a deterministic, real-space geometric trajectory model for bound hydrogenic atoms. By introducing the relativistic Lorentz mass variation directly into the scalar mass coefficient of Newton's Second Law ($F = m_{rel} \cdot a$), the system resolves into a periodic velocity wave equation rather than a traditional linear asymptote.

This structural realism models quantum mechanics not as an irreducible statistical paradox, but as the macro-temporal average of a hyper-fast $10^{17}$ Hz periodic electron clockwork operating under intense relativistic Coulomb field constraints.

By evaluating the pure Lorentz variant under the perpendicular geometric projections of a bound nucleus, the electron's velocity vector yields a stable wave equation:

$$v(t) = c \sin\left(\frac{Ft}{m_0 c}\right)$$

$$(m_e v^2)^2 + (hf)^2 = (m_e c^2)^2$$

$$E_n = \mu c^2 \sqrt{1 - \frac{Z^2 \alpha^2}{n^2}}$$
